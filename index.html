About

A human-centered
approach to data.

Connecting rigorous research with practical questions about technology, work, and well-being.

I'm a data scientist working at the intersection of human behavior, social impact, and emerging technologies. My career spans three interconnected areas.

01

AI and the workforce

Understanding AI's impact on people and business results: measuring productivity changes, skill shifts, and job displacement risks, and building frameworks to assess responsible AI implementation.

02

Mental health & well-being research

Using machine learning and behavioral data to understand mental health and everyday well-being, from patterns of depression and anxiety to changes in sleep, movement, and daily routines.

03

Data science for social impact

Studying how technology and social structures affect individuals and communities through network analysis, organizational decision-making, and the measurement of human behavior.

Across all three, I prioritize robust, interpretable models, production-ready systems, and research that informs real-world decision-making.
